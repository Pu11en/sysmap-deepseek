# References and external software

The optional viewer imports Mermaid 11.4.1 from jsDelivr at runtime. Mermaid is not bundled in this repository. Its source and MIT license are available at https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid/tree/v11.4.1 . Network access to that CDN is required for the viewer's drawing step.

The planning approach draws on ideas discussed in:
- Superpowers brainstorming: https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/main/skills/brainstorming/SKILL.md
- Anthropic context engineering: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents
- Anthropic agent evaluations: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents

This is an independent skill and implementation, not an installation or redistribution of those complete projects. It is not affiliated with or certified by those projects or any AI harness vendor.
